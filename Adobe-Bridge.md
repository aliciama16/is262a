# Adobe Bridge Lab
This is a guidance document for IS262A

If you have not done so already, download Adobe Bridge [https://www.adobe.com/products/bridge.html]. It is a free (and very helpful) program to download and use!

We're going to pretend that a researcher requested digital file access to this Soviet demobilization album in our institutional collection. Also known as a dembel album, it is a personal scrapbook created by a Soviet soldier at the end of their mandatory military service. They contain photographs, handwritten notes, sometimes illustrations and other decorations. We've asked a photographer to photograph the album and they've just sent us the files. Let's prepare this for researcher access!

# 1. Download the Class Files
Under Module 7

# 2. Unzip the folder 2010_010_001
* Once downloaded from Module 7, double click to extract/uncompress the folder
* You should now have a folder named 2010_010_001 with image files inside

# 3. Batch Rename the Files
We will rename the images uses their accession number followed by a sequential number

* Open Adobe Bridge
* Select "Libraries" from the workspace options at the top bar
* On the left-hand side, under "Folders," navigate to the downloaded & uncompressed 2010_010_001 folder.
* In the bottom panel box (where you see all your images appear), click within it once and press Control + A (Windows) or Command + A (Mac) to select all images
* Navigate to your computers top bar menu and select "Tools" -> "Batch Rename..." 
* Under "New Filenames" set up the filename elements:
* * Text: 2010_010_001_
* * Sequence Number: Starting Number 1; Select 3 digits  
* Press "Rename"
* Images should now all follow this sequence (2010_010_001_001, 2010_010_001_002, etc.)

# 4. Apply IPTC Core Metadata
We will add descriptive and administrative metadata to the images. Because the information applies to the entire group of images, we can create a metadata template and apply it to all of them at once.

* Click on "Metadata" at the top of Adobe Bridge
* Click on one of your images to highlight it so you see metadata pop up in the lower left corner
* Scroll down to the IPTC Core Section and take a look at the field options
* We want to apply the same IPTC metadata across images so we will go to our computers top menu and click "Tools" -> "Create Metadata Template"
* Name your template something like "Class Exercise - Dembel Album"
* Find the IPTC Core field and we will select and fill out the following fields:
* * Creator: Alicia Mara
* * Creator Country: USA
* * Creator Email: amara@wendemuseum.org
* * Keywords: Demobilization
* * Date created: (toggl on)
* * Credit Line: Courtesy of Wende Museum
* * Copyright status; unknown
* * Rights usage terms: Users are responsible for determining whether permission is required and obtaining it. Contact catalog@wendemuseum.org for information about known rights holders or additional rights information.
* Press Save

# 4.1 Apply the template to the images
* Click in the center box of bridge where all of your images are. Select all of your images and then select "Tools" from the top bar of your computer, and then "Append Metadata" and select the template you just made
* Watch Adobe Bridge go!
* If you have a Mac, go to Finder and search your computer for "Demobilization". Notice that even though the filename doesn't have that information, all the images appear in your search now

# 5. Create a PDF Access Sheet
* Select "Output" on the top bar of Adobe Bridge
* Look at the Output Settings box on the right. We will first change the document size. We will set dimensions of 29.7 for the Width and 20.99 for the Height.
* Click "include filename"
* Look further down to "Watermark" and open that section
* Select "Text Watermark"
* For text type in "Wende Museum (Researcher Name Here)"
* Under placement, type in 45 degrees for rotation, and select the center square.
* Make opacity 50%
* At the bottom, where all your images are, click into the box and select all your images (Control + A) and then drag them up to the white page in the center
* Click "Export to PDF" at the bottom right corner to export
